LABEL DEFINITIONS
-----------------
0 (ALIGNED) — the emotional tone of the voice matches what the words say:
    - Speaker sounds calm/neutral and says something neutral
    - Speaker sounds happy and says something positive
    - Speaker sounds frustrated and says something critical
    - The voice and the words tell the SAME emotional story

1 (INCONGRUENT) — the emotional tone of the voice CONFLICTS with the words:
    - "That's fine." said with an angry or tense voice
    - "I disagree." said in a cheerful, light tone
    - Sarcasm: enthusiastic words with flat or cold delivery
    - Polite or positive words with clearly tense vocal quality
    - The voice and the words tell DIFFERENT emotional stories

EDGE CASES:
    - Short fillers ("Okay", "Right", "Hmm") → almost always 0
      unless the tone is dramatically unexpected
    - Very short or partial words → if you cannot tell, label 0
    - Background noise → label based on what you can hear of the speaker

----------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT NOT TO JUDGE
-----------------
Do NOT evaluate:
    * Whether the idea or proposal is good or bad
    * Whether the speaker is correct or wrong
    * Whether the sentence makes logical sense
    * Whether the topic is interesting or boring

Only judge: does the voice TONE match the WORDS?

----------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT TO LISTEN FOR (tone checklist)
-------------------------------------
    Pitch:    high vs low
    Intensity: loud vs soft
    Speed:    fast vs slow
    Prosody:  rising/falling intonation, monotone
    Vocal cues: breathiness, tightness, laughter, sarcasm markers

Label 1 ONLY when the mismatch is CLEAR.
When in doubt, default to 0.
